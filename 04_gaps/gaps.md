# Candidate Gaps

## Pending Literature Verification

Candidate gap statements below are provisional until local RAG and full-paper evidence are checked.

1. Static soil-moisture threshold irrigation may ignore phenology-dependent water sensitivity.
2. Fuzzy irrigation studies may use soil-moisture/weather inputs without explicitly encoding dynamic paddy growth-stage target bands.
3. Simulation studies may report water saving or yield without enough target-band violation diagnostics by phenological stage.
4. Predictive/MPC irrigation needs a defensible stage-aware target trajectory; this project can become the controller-side module in the larger predictive-irrigation program.

## Required Evidence

Each candidate gap must be supported by repeated limitations, missing comparisons, weak baselines, or future-work statements from multiple sources before manuscript use.
