# Contribution Statement

## Draft Contribution, Not Yet Locked

This study proposes and evaluates a phenology-aware fuzzy irrigation controller for paddy rice that adapts soil-moisture target bands across growth stages and compares its closed-loop irrigation behavior against static/reactive baselines under weather-driven simulation.

## Claim Boundaries

- Do not claim field validation unless field data are added.
- Do not claim universal target-band values unless literature supports them directly.
- Do not claim algorithmic novelty in fuzzy inference unless the rule design or membership formulation is demonstrably new.
- It is safer initially to claim a controller formulation and evaluation protocol contribution.

## Evidence Needed

- Literature evidence for phenological water sensitivity.
- Literature evidence for static-threshold limitations or missing phenology-aware comparisons.
- Simulation evidence for water use, target-band tracking, and yield/IWUE outcomes.
